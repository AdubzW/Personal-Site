## Instructions

### Prep Work

1. Marinate paneer

   Cut paneer into medium cubes; toss gently with salt, pepper and garam masala and keep aside to absorb flavors.

   [![](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/marinated-paneer-cubes-on-a-plate-ready-to-roast.webp)](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/marinated-paneer-cubes-on-a-plate-ready-to-roast.webp)

2. #### Blanch and cool palak

   Blanch palak leaves cook briefly in hot water for 2 - 3 minutes and transfer immediately to cold water to keep color bright; drain.

   [![](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/bright-green-blanched-spinach-in-a-bowl-of-ice-water.webp)](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/bright-green-blanched-spinach-in-a-bowl-of-ice-water.webp)

3. #### Make palak puree

   Blend the cooled palak with coriander, green chillies and chopped ginger into a smooth, silky puree.

   [![](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/blender-jar-with-vibrant-green-palak-puree.webp)](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/blender-jar-with-vibrant-green-palak-puree.webp)

4. #### Soak and grind cashews

   Soak cashews until soft and grind into a smooth paste; set aside.

   [![](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/small-bowl-of-smooth-cashew-paste.webp)](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/small-bowl-of-smooth-cashew-paste.webp)

5. #### Chop tomato and ginger

   Chop the tomato and keep ginger ready for blending and cooking.

### Method

1. #### Roast paneer

   Heat a little ghee in a pan and lightly roast the marinated paneer until edges turn golden; remove and keep aside.

   [![](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/golden-edged-paneer-cubes-in-a-frying-pan.webp)](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/golden-edged-paneer-cubes-in-a-frying-pan.webp)

2. #### Warm oil and whole spices

   Heat oil and ghee together in a wide kadhai, add cinnamon, cardamom and cloves, then add cumin seeds and let them sizzle briefly.

3. #### Cook tomato base

   Add chopped tomato to the hot spices and cook until the tomato softens and breaks down.

4. #### Add dry spices

   Stir in turmeric, kashmiri chilli powder, cumin powder and salt; mix well with the tomato.

   [![](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/spices-being-stirred-into-the-tomato-base.webp)](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/spices-being-stirred-into-the-tomato-base.webp)

5. #### Add palak puree

   Pour in the palak puree and cook gently on medium-low, stirring so the greens and spices combine. Dilute them with little water and cook for 5 minutes.

   [![](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/rich-green-palak-gravy-simmering-in-pan.webp)](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/rich-green-palak-gravy-simmering-in-pan.webp)

6. #### Finish with cashew paste and paneer

   Add cashew paste, garam masala and amchur powder, then return the roasted paneer to the kadhai; simmer briefly to warm through and meld flavors.

7. #### Rest and serve

   Let the curry rest for 5 minutes, so the flavors settle, then serve warm with roti, naan or rice.

   [![](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/plated-palak-paneer-served-with-roti.webp)](https://homecookingshow.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/plated-palak-paneer-served-with-roti.webp)
